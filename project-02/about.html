About

Who I am

A software engineer with five years of experience building and shipping web applications.

I like the parts of the job that go beyond the code itself: breaking down an unclear problem, weighing trade-offs, and getting a team to a result it can stand behind.

Most of my work runs across the stack. On the back end that means designing APIs, modeling data, and keeping services reliable. On the front end it means building interfaces that are responsive, accessible, and easy to use. I try to write code that is clear enough for the next person to pick up without needing me in the room.

Over the past several years I have shipped features on real products, reviewed a lot of pull requests, and learned that the hardest part of engineering is usually communication, not syntax. Good software comes from a team that understands what it is building and why.

How I work

I lean on small, reviewable changes, automated tests, and clear commit history. I would rather ship something focused and well understood than something large and fragile. When requirements are fuzzy, I ask questions early instead of guessing.
